**Title**
Increment Item Quantity in Cart

**Related Requirement/User Story**
Cart quantity management functionality

**Date Created**
11-12-2024

**Author**
QA Team

**Priority**
High

**Preconditions**
1. User is on https://coffee-cart.app
2. At least one coffee item (e.g., Cafe Latte) has been added to cart
3. User has navigated to the cart page

**Test Steps**
| Step # | Step Description | Test Data | Expected Result |
|:------:|:----------------|:----------|:---------------|
|   1    | Navigate to cart page | N/A | Cart page displays with items |
|   2    | Verify "Cafe Latte" is in the cart | Coffee: Cafe Latte | Item is visible in cart list |
|   3    | Verify initial quantity | Expected: 1 | Quantity shows "$16.00 x 1" |
|   4    | Verify initial line item total | Expected: $16.00 | Total shows $16.00 |
|   5    | Locate the "+" button for Cafe Latte | N/A | Plus button is visible in unit controller |
|   6    | Verify button has proper aria-label | Expected: "Add one Cafe Latte" | Aria-label is present for accessibility |
|   7    | Click the "+" button once | N/A | Button click is registered |
|   8    | Verify quantity increments | Expected: 2 | Quantity shows "$16.00 x 2" |
|   9    | Verify line item total updates | Expected: $32.00 | Total shows $32.00 |
|   10   | Verify cart total updates | Expected: $32.00 | Pay button shows "Total: $32.00" |
|   11   | Click the "+" button again | N/A | Button click is registered |
|   12   | Verify quantity increments to 3 | Expected: 3 | Quantity shows "$16.00 x 3" |
|   13   | Verify line item total updates | Expected: $48.00 | Total shows $48.00 |
|   14   | Verify cart total updates | Expected: $48.00 | Pay button shows "Total: $48.00" |

**Postconditions**
Cafe Latte quantity is 3 in cart, total is $48.00

**Status**

**Environment**
