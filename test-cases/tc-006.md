|   8    | Click on "Flat White" coffee cup icon third time | Coffee: Flat White | Item added again |
|   9    | Verify cart count updates | N/A | Cart count shows "3" |
|   10   | Verify cart total updates | Expected: $54.00 | Pay button shows "Total: $54.00" |

|   13   | Verify quantity shows "3" | Expected: $18.00 x 3 | Quantity displays as 3 |
